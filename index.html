Nada Hatem is an English language trainer, IELTS specialist, content creator, and educator with a passion for turning language learning into a practical, confidence-building experience.

With a degree from the Faculty of Education, English Department, a postgraduate diploma in Teaching English as a Second Language, and professional TEFL/TESOL certifications, Nada supports learners with clarity, structure, and care.

She has worked with adult learners and students across IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, STEP, General English, and corporate communication. Her work is grounded in critical thinking, evidence, and the belief that your voice deserves room to grow.

01 What courses do you offer?+

IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, STEP, General English, Conversation English, and Corporate English training.

02 Are the courses online or in person?+

Courses are currently available online through live sessions, making them accessible to students inside and outside Egypt.

03 Are sessions private or group sessions?+

Both private and group options may be available depending on the course. Each course page or booking conversation will clarify the format.

04 Do I need to take a placement test?+

For General English and some exam-preparation courses, a placement or level assessment may be recommended to find the best starting point.

05 What IELTS score can I expect?+

Results depend on your current level, preparation time, practice, and individual performance. The course develops the skills and strategies needed for your target score, but no specific result can be guaranteed.
